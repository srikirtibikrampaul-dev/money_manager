# 5.2 Statement.md

## 1. Problem Statement

Managing personal income and expenses manually can be difficult. People may forget their spending details or find it difficult to understand where their money is being spent. The **Money Manager** is a Python-based personal finance tracking system that helps users record their income and expenses and monitor their financial status. The program calculates total income, total spending, remaining balance, and provides useful information about spending patterns.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The scope of the Money Manager project is to provide a simple command-line application for basic personal financial management. Users can add multiple income entries and record expenses under different categories such as Food, Transport, Shopping, Education, Entertainment, Bills, and Other. The system can calculate financial totals and provide summaries of recorded transactions. The current project focuses on basic expense tracking and does not include online banking, database connectivity, or automatic transaction tracking.

## 3. Target Users

The project is mainly designed for:

* Students who want to manage their monthly pocket money.
* Individuals who want to track their daily expenses.
* Beginners who want a simple personal finance management tool.
* Users who want to understand their spending habits and remaining balance.

## 4. High-Level Features

* **Add Income:** Record income amounts entered by the user.
* **Add Expense:** Record expenses along with their categories.
* **Total Income:** Calculate the total recorded income.
* **Total Spending:** Calculate the total amount spent.
* **Remaining Balance:** Display income remaining after expenses.
* **Category Summary:** Show spending for different expense categories.
* **Highest Expense:** Identify the highest recorded expense.
* **All Expenses:** Display all recorded expenses.
* **Financial Summary:** Provide an overall summary of income, spending, balance, and transactions.
* **Input Validation:** Prevent invalid or non-positive amounts and invalid category selections.
